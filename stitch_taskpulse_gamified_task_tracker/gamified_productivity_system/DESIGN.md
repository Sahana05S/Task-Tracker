## Brand & Style

This design system blends cheerful gamification with structured enterprise productivity. The design style fuses **Warm Modern Minimalist UI** with **Bento Box Modular Layouts** and subtle **Aurora Luminosity**. It rejects cold utilitarian software in favor of an inviting, optimistic workspace that celebrates human effort without feeling juvenile.

### Emotional Persona
- **Warm & Encouraging:** Transforms daily tasks into rewarding milestones without patronizing the user.
- **Clear & Structured:** High-contrast task details, compact Bento cards, and crisp micro-labels keep priority front and center.
- **Celebratory:** Utilizes friendly golden star motifs, soft lavender aura backdrops, and luminous mint completion indicators to make achievement tangible.

## Colors

The palette relies on soft, atmospheric pastels for macro surfaces and focused jewel tones for interactions, rewards, and feedback.

### Core Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F6FD` / `#F3F1FA`):** A soft, calming pale lavender canvas that eliminates screen fatigue.
- **Card Elevated (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure crisp white for elevated cards and Bento containers.
- **Primary Brand Violet (`#6355E6` / `#5846E0`):** Drives interactive hierarchy, prominent action buttons, and focused navigation states.
- **Recognition Gold (`#F5A623` / `#FFC043`):** Reserved for stars, achievement streaks, badges, and mascot highlights.
- **Progress Mint (`#10B981` / `#34D399`):** Indicates success, finished missions, active metrics, and healthy velocity.
- **Lavender Border Tint (`#EAE7F6`):** Delicate perimeter strokes on cards and inner list items.
- **Attention Amber / Peach (`#FFF9EB` / `#FFF1ED`):** Warning states, blocker panels, and "Waiting for help" alerts.

### Aurora Gradients
- **Hero & Mission Cards:** Linear transition from `#DCE5FF` via `#EAE4FC` to `#FFF3DF` (135deg).
- **Celebration Banners:** Soft multi-stop radial gradient blending vibrant lavender (`rgba(99, 85, 230, 0.08)`) with soft warm peach (`rgba(255, 192, 67, 0.12)`).

## Typography

The type scale is driven entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing balanced geometric curves with friendly human terminals that reinforce the welcoming, gamified tone.

### Typographic Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Bold or ExtraBold weights with tightened letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern punch.
- **Eyebrow & Section Badges:** Rendered with `label-uppercase`, set in full uppercase tracking (`+0.08em`), and tinted with primary violet (`#6355E6`) to guide scanning.
- **Body & Subtitles:** Muted dark lavender-gray (`#5C5776`) for supporting body copy ensures legibility while softening visual weight against dark headers (`#1A172E`).

## Layout & Spacing

The interface employs a responsive **Bento Grid System** configured to prioritize content bite-sizing and glanceable recognition.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max layout container bounded to `1200px` centered. 12-column layout with flexible Bento arrangements:
  - Hero summary banner spans 7 or 8 columns.
  - Quick-stats column spans 4 or 5 columns.
  - Multi-mission focus cards span across 7 or 8 columns with calendar and blocker cards on adjacent 4 or 5 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Major cards collapse to 4-column halves or full 8-column rows. Margin adjusts to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column fluid stack. Margin collapses to `1rem` with `gutter-mobile` set to `1rem`. All Bento grid cells reflow to 100% width.
- **Internal Density:** Base unit follows an 8px rhythmic grid with generous inner card padding (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) to retain an open, breathable workspace feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **low-contrast lavender outlines**, **crisp white surface elevation**, and **diffused ambient drop shadows**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat pale-lavender tint (`#F7F6FD`).
- **Level 1 (Bento Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` cards, 1px solid border (`#EAE7F6`), with shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(99, 85, 230, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 23, 46, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Items / Nested Task Rows):** Subtle elevated `#FBFBFE` surface with `border: 1px solid #ECE9F7` or soft violet active halo (`rgba(99, 85, 230, 0.12)`).
- **Level 3 (Interactive Floating Elements & Modals):** Drop shadow: `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(99, 85, 230, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 23, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Aurora Hero Elevation:** Employs soft multi-color backplates (`#DCE5FF` to `#FFF3DF`) with ambient internal glow instead of stark external shadows.

## Shapes

The geometric form follows roundedness tier `2` to balance friendly ergonomics with structured dashboard order.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Cards & Bento Panels:** Large rounded corner radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px) delivering soft, friendly framing.
- **Action Buttons & Badges:** Rounded pill buttons (`9999px`) for quick actions, tags, and category filters; moderate radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.75rem` / 12px) for primary CTA buttons.
- **Task List Rows & Inner Containers:** `rounded-lg` (12px to 16px) creating cleanly nested concentric radii within parent containers.
- **Avatars & Mascot Badges:** Circular (`rounded-full`) or smooth squircle avatars with soft lavender borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep violet fill (`#6355E6`), pure white text, 12px border radius, subtle hover lift (`#5846E0`), accompanied by trailing directional micro-arrow (`→`).
- **Subtle Action / Secondary:** Soft violet wash (`#EEECFC`), text in `#6355E6`, zero border, transitioning to `#E3DFF9` on hover.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent surface, hover background `rgba(99, 85, 230, 0.06)`, text in `#5C5776`.

### Priority Badges & Status Pills
- **High Priority:** `#FEECEB` fill with `#E53E3E` text and dot.
- **Medium Priority:** `#FEF3E2` fill with `#D97706` text and dot.
- **Low Priority:** `#E6F8F0` fill with `#059669` text and dot.
- **Filter Pills:** Active state uses soft lavender background (`#ECE8FA`) with bold violet typography; inactive state is transparent with subtle gray text.

### Progress Bars & Completion
- **Track:** 6px to 8px height, smooth rounded pill, `#EBE7F6` background.
- **Fill:** Bright Mint green (`#10B981` to `#34D399`) gradient, accompanied by crisp bold percentage text aligned to the right.

### Mascot & Gamification Elements
- **Golden Star Mascot:** Warm yellow-gold character (`#FFC043`) with subtle facial micro-expressions and four-point sparkle indicators.
- **Star Streak Calendar:** Days marked with solid circular or star badge badges (`#FFF0D4` fill with `#F5A623` star icon). Current day indicated by violet stroke badge.
- **Peer Recognition Chips:** Compact cards containing initial avatars, team member names, brief appreciation notes, and a gold star icon (`★ Contribution recognised`).

### Bento Task Cards
- Nested layout with leading category icon box (soft violet tinted `#F2EFFE`), title and priority badge top-left, full-width progress bar center, and contextual action button pinned right.
- Distinct status containers for blockers: soft peach background (`#FFFBF5`), delicate border (`#FFE6C7`), and high-urgency action button.
